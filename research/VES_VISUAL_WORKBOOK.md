# VES / PVC visual workbook

Date: 2026-10-08. Scope: EPS educational workbook at `/eps/#/ves`, built within the existing JavaScript/Vite runtime. No dependencies added. All geometry and waveforms were authored for this project. No external ECG image or patient recording is redistributed.

## Source decisions

- User-supplied [EasyECG Easy PVC](https://www.easy-ecg.com/diagnostic/dUBqGUnQNGjIZA907FT1) was inspected in the browser. Its visible questions covered V1 morphology, II/III polarity, precordial transition and lead I polarity. That reading sequence informs this workbook; its full decision tree, anatomical endpoints and images are not reproduced.
- The site's reference, [Nageler et al., EASY-PVC, ESC 2025 abstract](https://doi.org/10.1093/eurheartj/ehaf784.621), was independently located at the publisher. It is a congress abstract in *European Heart Journal*, 46 Supplement 1, not a validation study of this code. No published performance percentage is assigned to this workbook.
- [2019 HRS/EHRA/APHRS/LAHRS ventricular arrhythmia ablation consensus](https://doi.org/10.1002/joa3.12185) informs anatomical neighborhoods, broad origin groups and limits of surface localization. A region includes adjacent structures; a marker never establishes a safe ablation target. The quantitative local times below are invented teaching examples, not extracted from this document.
- [Betensky et al., 2011, V2 transition ratio](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jacc.2011.01.035): `[R/(R+|S|)]PVC / [R/(R+|S|)]sinus`, with ≥0.60 favoring LVOT in the studied LBBB/inferior-axis, V3-transition setting. This is displayed separately as an adjunct and does not overwrite anatomical candidates. No accuracy percentages are shown. Lead placement, sinus comparison and limitations are stated. Other morphology/axis/transition contexts and scar disable the calculator.
- [John, Ghazizadeh, Ceresnak, Heart Rhythm 2026, aortic root substrates](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.hrthm.2026.01.055) (local PDF supplied by the user) informs the opened aortic root view and the sinus texts: the true sources are the interleaflet triangles (ILT) and the muscle at the sinus bases, not the leaflets ("aortic cusp" is a misnomer); the right sinus overlies the septal summit, the non-coronary sinus the interatrial and membranous septum, the left sinus the aorto-mitral continuity; the RVOT infundibulum lies anterior to the R and L sinuses and the ventriculo-infundibular fold separates the posterior septal RVOT from the right sinus; R/L ILT PVCs are LBBB-like, inferior-axis with transition at V3 or earlier, an abrupt V2-V3 transition and a V1 downstroke notch; ILT potentials may precede QRS by 30-40 ms; GCV/AIV act as conduits and GCV ablation risks the diagonal branch; coronary ostia average 15-17 mm above the sinuses; an infra-aortic LAF branch lies beneath the right sinus. Drawings are original; the review's figures are not reproduced.
- [Clinical validation of automatic local activation time annotation during focal PVC ablation, Europace 2018](https://academic.oup.com/europace/article/20/FI2/f171/4587592) informs the paired bipolar/unipolar teaching concept: local bipolar activity versus surface QRS onset; unipolar departing-wavefront QS versus initial positive deflection away from a focus; maximal negative unipolar slope as an activation surrogate. QS is supportive, not definitive.

## Model and provenance

Data flow: authored region definitions → smooth synthetic beat (q, R and S Gaussian lobes, a notched R for the free-wall example, a gentle ST shift and a T wave discordant to the dominant QRS deflection; features measured only inside the 0..width QRS window) → measured V1 dominance, II/III polarity, sustained R/S transition, lead I and designed duration → broad candidate groups → selected anatomical teaching example → schematic local/reference recordings. ECG features are measured from the displayed example rather than inferred from the region name. Limb leads satisfy III = II − I and aVR + aVL + aVF = 0 at every sample. Precordial patterns are manually designed, not generated by a validated torso forward model. qR is available for manual reading, but no qR-specific validated classifier is claimed.

The 130 ms split is a teaching cue for relative QRS width, not an independent diagnostic threshold. Lead I is retained in the reading trail with qualitative context; it does not exclude candidates. V3 transition deliberately preserves RVOT/LVOT overlap. Contradictory patterns produce an unresolved state rather than a forced best-match percentage. Scar context suppresses idiopathic region suggestions and explains exit versus isthmus.

There are three schematic views: a basal valve-plane schematic, the aortic root opened at the L-N commissure (seen from the lumen, with sinotubular junction, ventriculo-arterial junction, virtual basal ring, R-L and R-N ILTs, sinus-base muscle, membranous septum, His, the infra-aortic LAF branch and the aorto-mitral curtain; examples 1, 3, 4 and 5 are placed on it) and a ventricular cutaway. The basal schematic marks the three ILTs at the commissures (muscular R-L, fibrous R-N and L-N) and the ventriculo-infundibular fold. Patient right is drawn to the viewer's left. In the basal view anterior is up: the RV outflow wraps in front of the aortic root from the tricuspid side to the pulmonary valve, which lies anterior and to the left of the aortic valve; the left main leaves the left coronary sinus, the LAD runs anteriorly and the LCx along the mitral annulus, and the LV summit sits in the angle between them. Annular examples sit on their rings (inferior tricuspid free wall, anterolateral mitral).

The basal view has a 3D toggle: `src/eps/assets/ves-basal-3d.webp` is rendered from the app's own `cardiovascular.glb` by `scripts/eps/render-ves-atlas.cjs` (atria, great vessels and thorax hidden, camera above the ventricular base; OrbitControls keeps its target, so the view is oblique). It is the surgeon's view, patient right on the viewer's right, the mirror of the schematic. Hotspots are projected through the same camera and then hand-adjusted onto the structures; the ventricular cutaway stays a schematic because the atlas is not modelled for sectioning. Diagram coordinates are not registered to `cardiovascular.glb`. The summit/CS and crux/MCV neighborhoods are named without claiming that distal CS samples GCV/AIV or MCV. His is a ventricular reference channel in the isolated PVC strip; its marked V is not an H potential. These strips do not depict complete sinus A-H-V conduction or prove a reentry mechanism.

Designed ABL bipolar onset relative to surface QRS onset:

| Teaching region | Near ABL V−QRS (ms) |
| --- | ---: |
| Septal RVOT | −32 |
| RVOT free wall | −28 |
| LVOT / aortic sinuses | −30 |
| LV summit | −24 |
| Para-Hisian | −25 |
| Tricuspid annulus | −31 |
| Mitral annulus | −29 |
| Posteromedial papillary muscle | −27 |
| Anterolateral papillary muscle | −26 |
| Left posterior fascicle | −22 |
| Moderator band | −26 |
| Cardiac crux | −23 |

Adjacent ABL onset is −9 ms and remote onset +18 ms in every example, solely to demonstrate timing change. Near unipolar is QS; adjacent/remote are rS. These deliberately idealized correspondences are not a universal biological rule or success threshold. Local Purkinje P is drawn 16 ms before local V only for the fascicle and moderator-band teaching examples. It does not establish focal versus reentrant mechanism. Sampling offsets in the diagram are illustrative, not distances in mm. Surface amplitude is relative; time is in ms, on one shared recording axis with QRS onset at zero. The 12-lead strip is drawn on ECG paper at 50 mm/s and 10 mm/mV with a 1 mV calibration pulse; the EP recording uses a 10 ms grid at 100 mm/s with per-channel colours (surface, His, reference and ABL pairs).

Selecting an anatomical example loads its labelled ECG, selects its anatomical view and resets sampling to near. Editing any finding clears the selected example and hides its ECG/recordings, preventing a stale example from appearing to validate manual input. Language and EPS tab changes preserve workbook state within the session. Reload returns to the default teaching example; the `#/ves` route persists. External source links sit in the source disclosure inside the module.

- `npm run test:ves-browser`: actual Chrome interactions through every example and recording; keyboard/focus; manual ratio entry; TR/EN state; scar guard; switching to WPW; direct route/reload; 320/390 px overflow; screenshots in `/private/tmp/cardia-ves-shots` by default.

Clinical expert validation remains outside the automated software tests. Numerical examples and schematic anatomy are explicitly marked as synthetic in the UI.
