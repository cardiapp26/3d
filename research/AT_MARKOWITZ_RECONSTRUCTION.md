# Atrial tachycardia teaching, Markowitz 2019

Source: supplied `Atrial tachycardia AER_Markowitz_FINAL.pdf`, Markowitz SM, Thomas G, Liu CF, Cheung JW, Ip JE, Lerman BB. *Atrial Tachycardias and Atypical Atrial Flutters: Mechanisms and Approaches to Ablation*. Arrhythmia & Electrophysiology Review 2019;8:131–137. DOI https://doi.org/10.15420/aer.2019.17.2; metadata verified with PubMed PMID 31114688.

Original designed teaching signals, not digitized source figures. No original figures/PDF bundled. Milliseconds are designed fiducial timestamps; amplitudes normalized, not calibrated mV. No drug dosing, energy, clinical response kinetics or ablation coordinates encoded.

Five examples:

1. Figure 1B principles: local atrial A 35 ms before surface P, negative schematic unipolar deflection. AH 80/HV 45 ms, AA 300 ms. The Gaussian U is a simplified negative signal, not a recovered clinical QS morphology.
2. Figure 1A principle: AT termination following adenosine marker. Termination not exclusive to AT; sinus/escape rhythm omitted, blank region does not imply asystole.
3. Automatic AT suppression/return: same atrial sequence pauses then returns, gap 1000 ms designed; no actual pharmacokinetic model.
4. Figure 1C–D principles: localized reentry, eight atrial cycles at 300 ms, last four conduct 2:1. ABL fragment deflections span 270 ms, 90% of TCL; separate event type prevents treating fragments as separate atrial activations. Long fractionation alone is not diagnostic, bystanders/pseudo-reentry explicitly acknowledged. AV ladder describes chamber timing, not localized atrial circuit geometry.
5. Atrial entrainment: baseline TCL 300 ms, abbreviated pacing train 240 ms, HRA capture 5 ms after S and remote ABL A 25 ms after S. PPI from last S 1540 to first unpaced HRA A 1860 = 320 ms; PPI−TCL = 20 ms. Fusion, multisite entrainment and high-density circuit map not modeled; one short PPI is insufficient to establish macroreentry.

Guide covers focal/macro/localized mechanisms; para-Hisian RA/NCC/LA comparison; full-cycle mapping, entrainment limits; bidirectional line block and mitral isthmus pseudoblock. Available in SVT and the existing focal/para-Hisian AT case panels. Existing patient case and algorithm findings unchanged by example selection.

Validation: event bounds, calipers, capture timing, AA versus VV, fragments excluded from beat counts, ladder block count and lack of AP/nodal return dependency, project checks/test/build, bilingual browser and mobile rendering.
